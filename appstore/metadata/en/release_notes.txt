You can now sign in to eUprava right from the app! Open the Token tab, tap "Sign in to eUprava" and enter your card's PIN. The app does the rest.

What else is new:

• You can now change your card's PIN and unblock it with the PUK code.
• Long-press the app icon for quick actions: read the card, print and sign in to eUprava.
• Automatic card reading now also works in the Token tab.
• Fixed bugs, improved the interface and made the app more stable.
